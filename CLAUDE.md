# ApiPipeline.NET

## Code comments

Every comment, single-line (`//`) or XML documentation (`///`), must be:

- Brief and to the point.
- Self-explanatory to a consumer of the package, with no need to know the code's history.
- Free of historical references or notes: no "previously", "used to", "was renamed", "no longer",
  "retired", "since 2.0", "fixed in", or links to reviews and issues.

History belongs in `CHANGELOG.md` and commit messages.
